- When the husband reinvests his ₹3.85 crore into another property to shelter his own gain, he pays stamp duty a second time.
- Had the wife simply bought a flat from an unrelated seller, the exemption would have worked the same way and stamp duty would have been paid only once. The speaker's conclusion is that the "clever" structure cost the couple money rather than saving it.
