## The three states described

- **Rising market.** Supply is below demand — fewer people selling than buying — and price moves up.
- **Consolidation.** The number of sellers matches the number of buyers, and price goes sideways.
- **Falling market.** Sellers increase and buyers decrease, and price starts to drop.
