## Why options exist, and what an option chain is

- Futures and options are derivatives: their price is derived from an underlying spot instrument, such as a single stock.
- Options were introduced as insurance. If you hold a stock in cash and it moves against you, a small premium paid for a put or a call caps the damage instead of leaving the loss open-ended.
- That use has largely been replaced by direct speculation — people now trade options and futures without holding the underlying at all.
- The guest's claim: almost all Indian traders work from candlestick patterns and indicators, very few even know option chain analysis exists, and that is why the losing majority stays the majority.

## What the video tells you to do

- Begin with one lot and one trade a day, and hold that size until results are consistent.
- Take no trade at all on a day when the market does not fit your method, even if that means six hours of watching.
- Learn by replaying past sessions in historical data rather than paying for the lesson live.
- Commit to one method for a full six months before judging whether it works.
