# Key points (V2) - rebuilt from English transcripts

These 35 videos were originally summarised from their Hindi caption
stream. Their transcripts have since been translated to English, so the
key points were rebuilt from the cleaner text. The first versions are
kept unchanged in `..\KEY-POINTS\` for comparison.

The other 92 videos were already in English and were not redone; their
only key-points files live in `..\KEY-POINTS\`.

**Numbers:** speech recognition routinely drops the leading digits of index
levels (24,800 becomes "800"). A level was reconstructed only where the
transcript confirms the full value somewhere; every reconstruction says so,
and unrecoverable figures were left out. Confirm any level against the video.
