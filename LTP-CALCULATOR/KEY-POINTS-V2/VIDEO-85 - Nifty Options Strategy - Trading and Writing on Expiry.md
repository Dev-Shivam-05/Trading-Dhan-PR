## The trade that is shown

## Why the speaker calls it a no-profit-no-loss trade

- If the market rallies hard past 24,800, the put side decays to nothing and hands over roughly ₹13, while the call leg is stopped out at ₹19 — a loss of about ₹13 from the ₹6 sale price. The two cancel out.
- If the market falls sharply instead, the call side goes to zero and the put leg is stopped at ₹19, producing the same cancellation.
- If the market simply stays between the two strikes and both premiums decay, the writer keeps the credit. The speaker does not walk through that outcome in this clip.
- On a violent move both legs can be stopped in turn, which is the worst case he acknowledges.

## What the video tells you to do

- Click Spot first, then AI LTP, so that premiums and the dotted Max Pain lines are visible before choosing strikes.
- Pick the strikes on either side of the Max Pain line rather than by eye.
- Set the stop loss at the sum of the two premiums collected, not at a percentage.
- Exit at about 25 paise instead of holding the position to zero.
