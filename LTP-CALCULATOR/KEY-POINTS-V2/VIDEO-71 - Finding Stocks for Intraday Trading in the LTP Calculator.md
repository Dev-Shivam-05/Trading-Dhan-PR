## The screening route inside the tool

- Click the symbol below the clock on the left of the screen to open **LTP Blast**.
- Inside LTP Blast, click **Show Nearest Stock** at the top right. This gives the list of candidate stocks.
- Open a stock and place the cursor on its first candle of the day. The tool then shows that stock's entry point, target and stop loss.
- Trades are labelled by point: P1, C1, C2. C points are the call/buy side, P points are the put/sell side.

## The rule being drilled

- If the stock's high has already crossed the target, or its low has already hit the stop loss, the trade is dead. Skip it.
- If neither the target nor the stop loss has been reached yet, it is an **open trade** and can still be entered.
- Read the direction correctly before judging. On a sell the stop loss is above and the target is below, so you check the high against the stop loss and the low against the target.
- Every point carries a risk number. A point with risk zero is takeable; the student was told to leave points showing risk of two, three or four.
- When two open trades compete, take the one with the lower risk number. ICICI Bank at risk zero was chosen over Bajaj Finserv at risk four.

## What the video tells you to do

- Screen from LTP Blast, not from a watchlist or from news.
- Always check the first candle of the day against the target and stop loss before considering an entry.
- Drop any stock where either level has already been reached. A used-up signal is not a trade.
- Prefer the lowest risk number among the open trades in front of you.
- Watch on the correct side of the trade: check the high when short, the low when long.
