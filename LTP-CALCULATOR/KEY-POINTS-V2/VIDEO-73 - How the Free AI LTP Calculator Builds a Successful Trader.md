## What the free chain marks up

- **Highest volume is labelled 100%.** The strike carrying the largest volume on the call side and on the put side is highlighted outright, instead of having to be spotted by eye in a column of numbers.
- **The runner-up is ranked as a percentage of it.** A second volume showing 99%, or an open interest showing 96%, tells you immediately how close the challenger is to the leader.
- **Open interest gets the same treatment** on both sides, along with the strike where open interest is changing.
- **A red imaginary line** marks where the spot price sits inside the chain.
- **Support and resistance are written out in words.** In the session shown, resistance is stated at 25,000 with WTT — week towards top, bullish pressure — sitting at 25,100, and support at 25,000 with WTB, week towards bottom, at 24,900.
- The speaker's argument is not that NSE hides this data. The same volume figures appear on both screens. It is that on the NSE chain you cannot see at a glance which number is the largest, and here you can.
