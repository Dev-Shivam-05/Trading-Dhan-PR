## The problem it addresses

- When you write options against the weekly range intraday, the market often jumps on day one or day two of the week and premiums rise sharply.
- That spike is when a writer is most tempted to abandon the position. The speaker's instruction is to sit still and trust the range instead of reacting to the premium.
- If you are writing at the L1 band, a large gap-up opening is the usual cause of the pressure.

## What the video tells you to do

- Do not exit a weekly-range write because premiums spiked on day one or day two; expect the spike and stay with the range.
- Recognise that L1 fails roughly a third of the time, so size an L1 write accordingly rather than treating it as a certainty.

## Worth knowing

- The speaker states plainly that these weekly and monthly range lines do not exist on any broker terminal or any other option chain, and that they are the LTP Calculator's own innovation. That is a product claim, and the clip is in large part a promotion for it.
- The 65 / 95 / 99 percent figures are the textbook one, two and three standard deviation proportions. The video asserts that the ranges behave this way in the market; it does not show any test or record.
