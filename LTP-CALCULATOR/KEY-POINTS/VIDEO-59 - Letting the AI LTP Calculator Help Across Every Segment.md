## The rules that eliminated most stocks

- Once a trade appears at C1, P1 is dead for the day, and vice versa. The same pairing applies to C2 and P2. Whichever comes first is the only one.
- If a stock opens directly at C2 or P2, that stock is untradable all day.
- If the target or the stop loss is reached inside the first or second candle, that line is untradable even though no entry was possible.
- If C1 and P1 print the same value, skip the stock entirely. This disqualified Petronet.
