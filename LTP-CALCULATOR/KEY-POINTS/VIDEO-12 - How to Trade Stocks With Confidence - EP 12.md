## The zero-LTP filter

- Before buying a Call, look down the in-the-money LTP column on the call side. If any of those values reads zero, skip the trade. The example shown ran 51, 43, 42, 37, 32, 28, 23, 13 — no zeros, so the price is tradeable.
- The same check applies in mirror image before buying a Put: no zeros in the in-the-money LTP column on the put side.
- The speaker notes that in stocks, unlike Bank Nifty, volume is often missing at many strikes, which is why these two checks matter more here.

## What the video tells you to do

- Wait until 9:25 AM before reading the option chain for the day.
- Identify whether support and resistance are on the same strike. If they are, expect a straight travel between the two extensions and no diversion.
- Take Call entries at the extension of support and Put entries at the extension of resistance, reading each from the opposite side's volume.
- Check the in-the-money LTP column on your side for zeros before entering.
- Prefer the first hit of the day. Reduce size or stand aside on later hits.
