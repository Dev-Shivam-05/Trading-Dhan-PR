## The three line modes explained

- **Live LTP** draws static lines at whatever the current extension of resistance, resistance +1, extension of support and support -1 are.
- **920** draws four fixed lines that lock in at 9:21 and do not move.
- **AI LTP** is the newer set that names the state and prints Risky, Moderate, Max Pain and Max Gain on each side.
- The lines visible at the open are the previous day's, because the volume that defines a level builds from zero each morning and cannot exist at 9:15.

## Expiry-day mechanics

- Implied volatility must reach zero on expiry day. Out-of-the-money options have zero intrinsic value, so only their time value is left, and that time value has to be extinguished.
- The speaker ties IV to the gap between spot and futures — around 15 points on the day shown. Spot and futures are forced to converge at expiry, and that convergence is what drives IV to zero.
- A rule introduced around February or March roughly doubles margin on expiry day. Either keep double margin in the account from the previous day or square off positions, as the speaker says he did.

## The game of percentages

- Look at the strike carrying both the highest volume and the highest open interest on each side, then at the weakness percentages beside them — about 70% weak-toward-bottom on resistance and 73% weak-toward-top on support in this example.
- When the day's resistance and support are only one strike apart, price usually closes at that strike. From that the speaker predicted a close near 23315, matching L1 of the weekly range.
- He was wrong, and said so on air. The market ran roughly 600 points up, made a high near 23871 and finished at L2 of the weekly range, around 23892.

## What the video tells you to do

- Stay neutral before the open. Do not decide in advance that you will be bullish or bearish; trade whatever the lines offer.
- Argue against your own trade. Actively look for reasons not to take it, and take it only if you cannot find one.
- Compare the stop loss on both sides before choosing, and reject any setup whose stop exceeds its target.
- When the AI says exit, exit. Do not average, hold or wait for a reversal because you think the state will flip back.
- After a stop loss, do not re-enter immediately. Wait for the next day.
- On expiry day, either carry double margin or close positions the day before.
