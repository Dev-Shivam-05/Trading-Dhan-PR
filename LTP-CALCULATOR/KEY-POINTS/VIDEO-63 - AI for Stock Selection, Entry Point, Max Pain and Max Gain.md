## The four lines on every stock

- The report is generated at 9:25 am, from the first five minutes of trading. Once drawn, the lines stay static for the day.
- From top to bottom: **P2, P1, C1, C2**. P2 and P1 are red and are sell levels; C1 and C2 are green and are buy levels.
- Sell when price reaches P1; if it pushes above P1, sell again at P2. Buy when price falls to C1; if it falls further, buy again at C2.
- Each line carries three numbers beside it — the entry, the **Max Pain** (stop loss) and the **Max Gain** (target).
- A separate bearish risk and bullish risk figure is shown at the top of the stock.

## The rules used to reject a stock

The speaker applied the same checks to every name and discarded most of them.

- **Risk must be 0 or 1.** Bearish risk of 2 or more kills the sell side; bullish risk of 2 or more kills the buy side. Rejected on this rule: Apollo Hospitals (bearish risk 3), Balkrishna Industries (bullish risk 5), Hindalco (risk 2), ATGL (risk 2), Concor (risk 2), Kalyan Jewellers (risk 4).
- **All four lines must be visible.** SAIL, Bharti Airtel and Idea showed only three lines and were dropped.
- **The stock must not open at the outer line.** Titan and HDFC AMC opened below C2 and were discarded — an open at C2 or P2 makes the stock untradable for the day.
- **The stop must not already be hit.** Indigo's first-candle low of 5,281 was below the C1 Max Pain of 5,281.40, so the stop had been touched before entry.
- **The target must not already be hit.** ICICI Bank's low of 1,405.50 had passed its 1,405.30 target; Axis Bank's low of 1,217.40 passed a Max Gain of 1,218.78; Infosys' first-candle high of 2,701 passed a C1 Max Gain of 2,696. All rejected.
- Once a stock trades and hits its target on C1, P1 is no longer traded that day — only P2 or C2 remain.

## What the video tells you to do

- Open the LTP Calculator menu, go to Reports, then LTP Blast, and wait for the 9:25 am list. Use "Show Nearest Stock" to cut the list down.
- Check risk first, then line count, then the first candle's high and low against Max Pain and Max Gain, before considering any entry.
