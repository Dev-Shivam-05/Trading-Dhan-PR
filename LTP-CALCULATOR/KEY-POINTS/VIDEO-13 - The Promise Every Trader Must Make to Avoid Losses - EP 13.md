## The idea behind Chart 1.0

- In the option chain, call data on the left generates resistance and put data on the right generates support.
- Each of those two levels can be in one of three conditions: strong in itself, weak towards the top, or weak towards the bottom. Three conditions for support times three for resistance gives nine scenarios.
- The nine scenarios are said to expand later into 121 combinations, which the speaker defers to a future episode.
- Chart 1.0 is reached through the saffron **COA** button at the top right of the LTP Calculator screen.
- On that screen support is drawn above and resistance below — deliberately inverted from a price chart — which the speaker flags as a point of confusion to watch for.
- Reading the arc marks: an **upward-facing arc means weak towards the bottom**, and a downward-facing arc means weak towards the top. The speaker stresses this because it is counter-intuitive.

## What the chart tells you

- Two columns sit beside the levels. "Extension of Support" tells you whether a Call is allowed today. "Extension of Resistance" tells you whether a Put is allowed today. A column marked red means do not take that side.
- Scenario 1 — support strong, resistance strong: both sides are allowed. Puts from the extension of resistance, Calls from the extension of support. The first attempt is safest and risk rises with each repeat hit.

- Scenario 5 — resistance strong, support weak towards the top: prefer Calls.

## The promise in the title

- He states this framework is aimed at the conservative or swing-oriented trader.
