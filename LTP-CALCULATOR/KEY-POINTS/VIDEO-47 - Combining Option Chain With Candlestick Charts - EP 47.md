## The rules stated along the way

- Resistance weak towards bottom means bearish pressure. Support weak towards bottom, once it strengthens, means bullish pressure.
- Both pressures bearish is a blood bath. Both bullish is a bull run.
- A state of confusion needs at least an hour on the clock before it counts, and ends as soon as the opposite side stops being strong. The threshold given for that side losing strength is a percentage reaching **75**.
- Trading an extension of resistance while the resistance percentage is climbing back is dangerous, because there is no sensible place to put a stop loss and the position gets cut for no reason.

## What the video tells you to do

- Wait for price to reach the extension of support before buying. Do not buy at immediate support, even though a community member reported doing it profitably with a 10–15 point stop.
- In a bull run, do not sell. In a blood bath, do not buy.
- If price falls to extension of support minus one, average the position; if support then goes weak towards bottom, exit.
- Watch the opposite side's percentage while in a state-of-confusion trade. Once it reaches 75, the state of confusion is over and so is the trade.
- Accept days like this one. The speaker chose this session deliberately to show that correct entries, a correct average and a correct exit can still produce almost no money.
