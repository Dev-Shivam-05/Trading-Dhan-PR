## Why the speaker calls it a no-profit-no-loss structure

- If the market runs up past 24,800 and keeps going, the put premium decays to zero and you keep about ₹13, while the call leg is stopped out at ₹19 for a ₹13 loss. The two cancel.
- If the market falls hard, the call premium goes to zero and you keep about ₹6, while the put leg is stopped out at ₹19 for a ₹13 loss. Again the two roughly cancel.
- The profit case is the market staying between the two strikes so both premiums decay to near zero by close.
