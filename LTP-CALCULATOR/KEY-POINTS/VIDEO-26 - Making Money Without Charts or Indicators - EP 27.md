## The style of trade being described

## Reading the chain for a positional trade

- The tool is the Swing tab reached from the home button inside the LTP Calculator.
- For positional work, read Open Interest, not volume. Volume is treated as an intraday measure only.
- The highest Open Interest on the Put side marks support; the highest on the Call side marks resistance. A second, impactful Open Interest reading is shaded yellow.
- Weak towards top and weak towards bottom are judged from Open Interest in this mode.
- The distance between the support strike and the resistance strike is the expected move — about ₹10 in both examples.

## The check that decides the trade

- The yellow band alone is not enough. You have to open the historical data and find out where that Open Interest came from.
- If the Open Interest has moved up into the strike from a lower one, the support is genuinely strengthening and the buy is good.
- If it is drifting down towards a lower strike it is going the wrong way, and the "support" is a trap.
- Apollo Tyres on 5 November: Put-side Open Interest highest at 380 with Call-side at 390, a roughly ₹10 range, but the support was weak towards bottom and preparing to shift to 370. Walking back through 2 and 3 November showed the Open Interest had not previously held at 370 — it was heading down, so the setup is labelled a trap and skipped.
- Federal Bank, a position the speaker says he took himself: on 4 November support at 140, resistance at 150. Going back to 27 October, 140 was weak towards bottom in the morning, but through the day the weakness percentage fell and the yellow lightened, meaning the support was strengthening.
- Federal Bank made lows near 138 on 27 October and near 137–138 the next day. He states he bought around 138.8–140, the stock was at 143 at recording — about ₹5 — and his target is 150.
- The entry value itself is taken by clicking the volume at the extension of support.

## What the video tells you to do

- Screen positional candidates from the Swing tab, then confirm each one in the historical data before entering.
- Reject any setup where the Open Interest is migrating towards a lower strike, however strong the support looks today.
- Watch the weakness percentage over a session: falling weakness with a lightening yellow shade is the confirmation that the level is firming up.
- Enter at the extension of support and hold towards the resistance strike.
