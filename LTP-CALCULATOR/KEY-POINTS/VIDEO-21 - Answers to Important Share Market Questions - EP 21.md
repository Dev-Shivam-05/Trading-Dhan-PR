## Why the levels stopped calculating

- All the levels are derived from Option Greeks, so when the Greeks move fast the level values become unreliable.
- Implied volatility around the imaginary line sat stable near 9.6–9.9 from 9:30 until about 10:30.

## Reading shifting as a process, not an event

- Shifting is complete only when the volume difference between the old and the new support strike closes to near zero. Until then, the level has not moved.
- The difference through the day: about 8 lakh at 12:00, about 6 lakh at 12:15 (33 lakh against 27 lakh), still about 5 lakh at 12:30 when the caller entered.
- By 1:10 the two strikes were about 35.91 lakh and 34.68 lakh — roughly 1 lakh apart. By 1:20 the gap was down to about 10,000, so shifting was effectively done.
- With shifting complete, the correct entry was the divergence of the new support's extension — the Call-side value of the 19,700 strike, which read about 19,708–19,709. A line drawn there held: the market topped out near 19,725 around 2:00 and fell away from it.

## What the video tells you to do

- Do not act on an expected shift while the volume difference between the two strikes is still large. Wait until the gap closes, then take the trade.
- Once shifting is complete, take the entry at the divergence of the extension of the new support, not at the old level.
- Check implied volatility around the imaginary line before trusting a level. Stable implied volatility means the levels reverse the market accurately; rising implied volatility on both sides means they will not.
- Back-test any method yourself 10 to 20 times before putting real money on that value.
- Stay on a single lot for six months to a year rather than doubling size to make money faster.
