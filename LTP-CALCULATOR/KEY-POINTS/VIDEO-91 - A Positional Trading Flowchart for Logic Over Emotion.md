## The bullish branch

- This setup is for cash and futures only. The video says explicitly not to do naked option buying on it.
- Shifting Status must read either **Strong** or **WTT** (weak towards top).
- The Put High-OI Reversal must be either in breakdown, or sitting level with the current market price.
- Star rating must not be more than zero or one.
- If all three conditions match, buy at the current market price.
- Target: the value shown at the Call High-OI Reversal, or the strike carrying 100% open interest.
- Stop loss: triggered if the put side's 100% open interest turns **WTB** (weak towards bottom).

## The bearish branch

- Shifting Status must read **Strong** or **WTB**.
- The Call High-OI Reversal must be in breakdown or close to the current market price.
- Star rating again must not exceed zero or one.
- If all match, sell at the current market price.
- Target: the strike where the Put High-OI Reversal shows 100% open interest.
- Stop loss: triggered if the call side's 100% open interest turns weak.

## How the hedge is sized

- For a long position, subtract the Put High-OI Reversal from the Call High-OI Reversal. Buy a put whose premium is roughly 5% to 7% of that difference.
- Buy it for the same quantity as the position: the number of shares held, or the number of futures lots.
- Worked example: holding 1,000 shares or a 1,000 lot, the put bought works out around ₹10 to ₹17 of premium. That premium is effectively the stop loss for the whole trade.
- For a short position, the mirror applies: subtract the Call High-OI Reversal from the Put High-OI Reversal and buy a call at roughly 5% to 7% of that difference.

## What the video tells you to do

- Check all three conditions on the flowchart before entering. One failing condition means no trade.
- Use the current market price as the entry, not a level further away.
- Buy the hedging option at entry, and treat its premium as your loss limit instead of placing a price stop.
- Match the flowchart terms against the report table sitting beside it, which displays Call and Put High-OI Reversal, Shifting Status (Strong, WTT, WTB), star rating and CMP.
