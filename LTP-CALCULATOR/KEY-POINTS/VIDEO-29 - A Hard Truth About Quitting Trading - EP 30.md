## What the video tells you to do

- Never enter at the running price. Pick the level, wait for price to arrive there, and let the trade come to you.
- When a level shifts, restart the one-hour clock from the moment the shift completes, not from the open.
- Write out the possible paths for the day in advance and trade whichever one the chain confirms.
- Recalculate risk and reward whenever price reaches a level; a bullish trade can become a bearish one purely because the remaining upside has shrunk.
- Take no new trades after 2:30 pm.
