## When averaging is legitimate

- Averaging is only valid when the situation you entered on is still the situation on screen. You are bearish, the market is still bearish, and price has simply come a little above your entry.
- If the pressure has turned against you, adding a lot is not averaging. It is building a second position against yourself on top of one already trapped.
- Price sliding towards a line is not a reason to add. The reason to add is that the readings still favour you.
- The speaker frames this as eligibility: you earn the right to average only once you can read the percentage pressure and say what would end the current scenario.

## What the video tells you to do

- Never open a position at S Max Gain. That is the target; wait for S Moderate.
- Before entering, calculate what the trade loses at its stop loss with the number of lots you intend to hold.
- Exit when the scenario flips, not when you run out of patience. Both trades reviewed had a clean exit window that was ignored.
- Choose strikes near the money rather than deep in the money.
- Keep a written log of losing trades with date and time, replay each one in the app's historical data at speed, and name the specific mistake.
