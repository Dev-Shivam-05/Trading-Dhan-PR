## Two ways to set the stop loss

- **Fixed at 100%.** Hold the trade until the percentage reaches 100 and treat that as the stop. Outcome is roughly a coin flip: price may end at your cost, at S Max Pain, or run to R Risky / R Moderate / S Max Gain.
- **Percentage stop.** Exit at a chosen percentage level before 100 — 80, 90, 95, 98. The speaker's claim is that exiting before 100% usually ends at cost or slightly better.
- Which of the two to use is a personal risk decision, not something the tool decides.
