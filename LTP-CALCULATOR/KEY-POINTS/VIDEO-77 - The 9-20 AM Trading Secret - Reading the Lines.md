## What the 920 button does

- The button sits on the right-hand side of the chart, above the price panel.
- Clicking it draws the 9:20 lines. Despite the name, they are generated at **9:21 am**, once the first few minutes of trade have been read.
- They are **static**. Once drawn they never change for the rest of the session, and they show the same values when you open that date later in historical data.

## The four levels

- **US-1**: 24,529 in the example shown.
- **EOS** (extension of support): 24,575.
- **EOR** (extension of resistance): 24,620.
- **UR+1**: the top line. The figure is garbled in the transcript.
- Together these four form what the speaker calls the day's range.

## The claim being made

- The theory is that once the market reverses off these levels and completes its targets, that is the range the day will trade in.
