## What an Option Chain is

- The claim opening the session: barely one percent of Indian traders know that Option Chain analysis exists. The majority still trade candlestick patterns and indicators.
- Futures and options are derivatives - their price is derived from the spot price of the underlying stock or index.
- Options were originally created as insurance for a cash holding. If you own a stock and it moves against you, a put or a call caps the damage for a small, limited payment.
- The worked illustration: a stock trading at 2,800 that you expect to fall to 2,000 but which instead rises to 3,000. A small premium covers that adverse move.
- Over time the use changed. Traders now buy and sell options and futures directly, with no underlying cash position to protect.

## The rules that produced consistency

- One lot only, and one trade a day, held to for six months.
- Never take a trade against the direction the data shows. Take only the setups classified as safe.
- Copy the method exactly rather than adapting it - the guest describes copying "to the point", including how the host thinks about a setup.
- Sit out entirely when nothing fits. Both speakers describe watching the market for six hours and taking no trade at all.
- Overtrading was the early trap: on four days of overtrading, three ended in a loss. The stated priority became avoiding losses rather than chasing profit.
- Results claimed: runs of 40 to 50 trading days without a losing day, with a best run of 63 consecutive trading days - roughly three months. Audience members quoted runs of 22 days and 230 to 238 days.
