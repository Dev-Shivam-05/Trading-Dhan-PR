## Why stocks rather than the indexes

- The speaker's position is that Nifty and Bank Nifty are entertainment for intraday, and real money is built in stocks.
- Expiry days drag the indexes into consolidation, so a trader with only index positions has nothing to work with on those days.
- Stocks therefore need to be part of the toolkit, but with roughly 200 F&O names the problem is filtering them fast enough to act in the live market.

## The scanner tabs

- The LTP Calculator home screen carries four stock tools, spoken as IDD Dips, IDD Picks, Rally Stocks and IDD Swing.
- This episode covers IDD Picks only. It runs on the live market and returns the stocks currently sitting at their support or resistance - the ones that can move today.
- The output is split into bullish (stocks parked at their bottom, ready to rise) and bearish (stocks parked at their top, ready to fall).

- The filter only narrows the field. The analysis on each name is still the trader's own work.
- Stocks show a monthly range in the Option Chain, unlike Nifty and Bank Nifty which show a weekly one.

## The three rules for a stock trade

1. **Level type.** To buy a call, neither the support nor the resistance may be weak towards bottom. To buy a put, neither may be weak towards top.
2. **Zeros on the in-the-money side.** Before buying a call, check the in-the-money call LTPs; before buying a put, check the in-the-money put LTPs. One zero is tolerable, more than one or two makes the trade dangerous. A price displayed as 37.1 is not a zero - only a zero value counts.
3. **Implied volatility.** The IV around the at-the-money strike should sit around 20 and must not exceed roughly 25 to 28. Above that, accuracy and reversal quality break down.

All three must pass. One failure cancels the trade.

IV readings were 20-21 and only a single zero appeared on the in-the-money call side, so all three rules passed.

- **Ashok Leyland (bearish).** Both support and resistance strong with no weak-towards-top reading, IV around 21-22, and no zero on the in-the-money put LTP. All three rules passed, so the put buy is allowed.
