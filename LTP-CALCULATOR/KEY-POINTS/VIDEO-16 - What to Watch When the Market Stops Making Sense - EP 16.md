## Reading the gap at the open

- A change of 100 points in the LTP change column on both sides means a large gap. Anything up to 15 or 20 points is normal.
- Colour tells the direction: call side green with put side red means a gap up; call side red with put side green means a gap down.
- On this day the chain showed a large gap up. Nifty opened around 19,580–19,590, putting the imaginary line between 19,550 and 19,600, with support at 19,500 weak towards the bottom and resistance weak towards the top.

## How the day actually ran

- Support weak towards the bottom with resistance weak towards the top is scenario eight of the Chart of Accuracy. The support then shifts, and the market stops at the divergence one strike above the weak level — 19,520 here. The market fell to 19,520 and stalled there for about 30 minutes.
- The rules flip once shifting has actually happened. Before shifting, resistance weak towards the top targets that level minus one strike and support weak towards the bottom targets that level plus one strike. After shifting, the targets are read off the new extension of resistance or new extension of support instead.
- With support shifted down and resistance still pulling up, the two sides were pulling against each other. The new extension of support at 19,470 became the floor.
- Around 10:00 to 10:25 the resistance itself shifted down from 19,600 to 19,500 — visible because the volumes at both strikes were almost equal, near 101,000. That looked like heavy bearish pressure and produced a 25–50 point short down to about 19,470.

## The state of confusion

- Definition: when support or resistance cannot settle on one strike for an hour, that side is in a state of confusion. Here the resistance had been undecided for 113 and then 140 minutes.
- Rule: once the opposite side is strong, the market moves *towards* the confused side. Support strong plus resistance confused means the market turns bullish, however bearish the open interest and volume clustered at 19,500 looks.
- The speaker's point is that this is exactly where people reading the option chain get trapped — the chain looks like a bloodbath while the market is about to rise.
- A normal weak-towards-top stops one strike below the weak level. A state-of-confusion weak-towards-top does not follow that limit; how far it can run is held back for the paid community content.

## What the video tells you to do

- Book the short and flip to the call side once the confusion is established with the other side strong. Targets that day were the 19,566 divergence, then 19,614 at the weak-towards-top strike.
