## The questions to run before a trade

- Target versus stop loss: how many points is each, and what is the ratio between them?
- Is the chart pointing in a direction that favours the trade, or not?
- If the scenario changes, does it change in your favour or against you?
- If the market is in a State of Confusion, what direction is likely to follow?
- Is the momentum already complete, or is there still move left in it?
- Is the percentage reading increasing or decreasing? This only applies where a percentage is available.

## How to apply them

- The same list of questions is asked four times over, once for each part of SWOT: strengths, weaknesses, threats and opportunities.
- The speaker's claim is that asking these questions of yourself, in that repeated way, is the whole of the analysis. Nothing else is required.
