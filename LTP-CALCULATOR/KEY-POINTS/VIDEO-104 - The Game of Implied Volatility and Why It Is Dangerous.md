## What the speaker says this means

## What the video tells you to do

- Before trading, check implied volatility on both the call and put side and compare them.
- Treat a wide or swinging gap between the two as a reason to stay out rather than as noise.
- Watch the spot-to-futures difference during the session. A narrowing gap means the futures leg will not deliver the move that spot is making.
