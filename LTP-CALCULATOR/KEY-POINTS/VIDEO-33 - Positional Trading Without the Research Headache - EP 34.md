## What the video tells you to do

- Locate the highest put Open Interest for the floor and the highest call Open Interest for the ceiling, then buy near the floor with the ceiling as target.
- Treat the strike where the highest Open Interest sits as a wall, and the move of that Open Interest to a higher strike as permission for price to follow it.
- Watch the colour intensity of a blocking strike intraday. Darkening means the level is holding; fading means the pressure is coming off and price can move through.
- When Open Interest leaves a level and rebuilds one strike higher, expect the old resistance to become the new support.
- Exit at the strike the crowd has moved to, rather than holding through it.
